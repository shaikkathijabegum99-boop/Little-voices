FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About Our Pricing

Find answers to common questions about therapy sessions, assessments, and service fees.

How much does a therapy session cost?

Fees may vary depending on the service, session duration, and your child's needs. Please contact us for current pricing and availability.

Is an initial assessment required?

An initial discussion or assessment may help determine your child's needs and appropriate next steps. Contact our team to confirm the process for your chosen service.

Do you offer therapy packages?

Please contact us to ask whether ongoing session plans or packages are currently available and which options may suit your family.

How can I book an appointment?

Visit our contact page to send an enquiry. Our team can discuss your concerns, available appointments, and relevant fees.

Will my child need ongoing therapy?

The recommended frequency and duration of therapy depend on an individual assessment, your child's goals, and their progress over time.

Need Help Choosing a Service?

Every child's journey is different. Contact us to discuss your concerns, learn about available services, and ask about current fees.
