UNDERSTANDING COMMUNICATION

Every Child Has a Unique Voice

Communication develops in different ways. Recognizing your child's strengths is the first step toward helping them express themselves confidently.

Sounds & First Words

Discovering sounds, trying new words, and learning to express wants and needs.

Listening & Understanding

Learning to understand words, follow directions, and respond to everyday communication.

Connecting With Others

Connecting with others Developing turn-taking, sharing ideas, and participating in conversations.

Gestures & Expression

Using gestures, facial expressions, and other communication methods to share meaning.

GROWING THROUGH EVERY STAGE

A Journey Through Communication

Explore examples of communication skills children may develop as they grow. Every child progresses at their own pace.

0–12 MONTHS

Early Connections

Responding to sounds, making cooing and babbling sounds, and engaging through facial expressions.

1–2 YEARS

First Words

Beginning to use meaningful words, communicate needs, and understand familiar instructions.

2–4 YEARS

Growing Conversations

Combining words, asking questions, describing experiences, and developing clearer speech.

4+ YEARS

Sharing Ideas

Expressing thoughts, telling stories, and participating in increasingly complex conversations.

These are general examples, not diagnostic criteria. If you have concerns about your child's communication, consult a qualified speech-language professional.

CARE THAT STARTS WITH LISTENING

A Personalized Path to Progress

Every child is different. We focus on understanding individual strengths, communication needs, and family priorities.

01

Listen & Understand

Discuss your concerns, your child's strengths, and your family's goals.

02

Assess & Plan

Develop an individualized plan based on professional assessment and your child's needs.

03

Practice Through Play

Build communication skills through engaging, meaningful activities.

04

Grow Together

Review progress and help families support communication during everyday routines.

LITTLE MOMENTS MATTER

Encourage Communication Every Day

Simple, enjoyable interactions can create more opportunities for children to listen, express themselves, and connect with others.

01

Read Together

Look at pictures, name familiar objects, and talk about what happens in the story.

02

Make Play Meaningful

Follow your child's interests and take turns during enjoyable play activities.

03

Talk About Everyday Life

Describe daily routines and give your child time to respond in their own way.
