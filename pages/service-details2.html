UNDERSTANDING THE SERVICE

How can this support help?

Language includes both understanding what others say and expressing messages. Some children need extra support with vocabulary, sentence structure, answering questions, or following everyday instructions.

Every person's communication profile is different. A qualified speech and language therapist can discuss concerns and help determine whether an assessment or support plan may be appropriate.

Support that starts with the individual

Language support focuses on practical communication so children can understand more, express themselves, and connect with others.

    PERSONALISED SUPPORT

    Understanding Words, Ideas, and Sentences

    Language includes understanding what others say and using words, phrases, and sentences to share ideas. Support may focus on vocabulary, concepts, following directions, answering questions, telling stories, or combining words in ways that are useful in daily life.

    There is no single approach that suits everyone. Support is planned collaboratively, with attention to the individual’s preferences, strengths, culture, and everyday environments.

    • Goals chosen around real-life needs
    • Encouraging, respectful activities
    • Regular opportunities to review progress
    SUPPORT BEYOND THE SESSION

    Making Language Part of Everyday Life

    Books, pretend play, cooking, shopping, and conversation can provide natural opportunities to practise language. Families may learn ways to model words, expand a child’s message, offer choices, and allow enough time for a response.

    Progress can look different for each person. The therapist and family can review what is working, adjust activities, and identify useful next steps together. Questions and feedback from caregivers are welcome throughout the process.
