COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions Parents Often Ask

Here are a few helpful answers before you contact us.

How do I book an appointment?

Complete the enquiry form or contact the center by phone or email. Our team can help you arrange a suitable consultation time.

What should I expect during the first consultation?

The initial conversation helps us understand your concerns, your child's communication needs, and possible next steps. The exact process depends on your service.

Can parents participate in therapy?

Family participation can be an important part of supporting communication skills. Your therapist can discuss suitable activities to practise at home.

Do I need a referral to contact the center?

Contact the center to confirm its referral requirements and what information, if any, you should bring to your first appointment.
