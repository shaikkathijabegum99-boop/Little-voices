COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

A few helpful answers for families considering speech and language support.

When should I consider speech and language therapy?

Consider seeking advice if you have concerns about speech clarity, language understanding, expressing needs, fluency, or social communication. A qualified professional can help you decide whether an assessment may be useful.

How do I know which service is right for my child?

Start by sharing your concerns with a qualified speech and language therapist. An assessment, when appropriate, can help identify strengths, needs, and possible goals.

Can parents participate in therapy?

Family involvement can be valuable. Therapists may share practical strategies and activities that families can use during everyday routines, depending on the individual's needs and therapy plan.

How can I book an appointment?

Contact our team through the contact page to ask about available services, appointments, and the next steps for an initial consultation.
