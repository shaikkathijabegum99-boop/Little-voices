UNDERSTANDING THE SERVICE

How can this support help?

Communication is part of daily routines at home, school, work, and in the community. Practising useful messages in familiar situations can make skills easier to apply beyond a therapy session.

Every person's communication profile is different. A qualified speech and language therapist can discuss concerns and help determine whether an assessment or support plan may be appropriate.

Support that starts with the individual

Functional goals focus on helping each person participate more fully in the activities and relationships that matter to them.

Areas of support

  • Requesting help, objects, or information
  • Making choices and communicating preferences

  • Sharing experiences and personal information
  • Practising communication in familiar routines

    PERSONALISED SUPPORT

    Communication That Supports Daily Independence

    Functional communication focuses on messages that matter in daily life, such as asking for help, making choices, expressing discomfort, greeting others, asking questions, and sharing information. Goals are chosen around the person’s routines and priorities.

    There is no single approach that suits everyone. Support is planned collaboratively, with attention to the individual’s preferences, strengths, culture, and everyday environments.

    • Goals chosen around real-life needs
    • Encouraging, respectful activities
    • Regular opportunities to review progress
    SUPPORT BEYOND THE SESSION

    Practising Across Home, School, and Community

    Skills can be practised with familiar objects, visual supports, role-play, and everyday routines. Families and other communication partners can help by creating opportunities, allowing response time, and acknowledging all meaningful attempts to communicate.

    Progress can look different for each person. The therapist and family can review what is working, adjust activities, and identify useful next steps together. Questions and feedback from caregivers are welcome throughout the process.
